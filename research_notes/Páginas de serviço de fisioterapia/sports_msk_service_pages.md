# Sports / MSK physiotherapy service pages: structure, content and real examples (as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: primary evidence = real clinic service pages fetched on 2026-10-30 (UK, USA, Australia, Spain, Portugal, plus one Brazilian page included only because it is a Portuguese-language, WhatsApp-first booking model directly relevant to Fisio4you). Section orders below are as they appeared to the page fetcher; exact button counts are approximate where sites repeat CTAs in header, mobile bar and footer.

## 1. What sections do top sports/MSK service pages include, and in what order?

### Takeaway
The strongest pages follow a fairly consistent spine: **Hero (service + promise + 1 booking CTA + rating) → who it is for / problem → what we do (stages or sub-services) → how it works / what to expect (numbered steps) → objective testing / equipment → proof (stats, testimonials, partner logos) → team → pricing/duration → FAQ → closing booking block + location**. The pages that feel most "premium" are the ones that commit to a single flagship condition or service (e.g. ACL, return-to-sport testing) and go deep, rather than listing everything.

### Inferences
- A defensible default order for a Fisio4you sports/MSK page: Hero (what + for whom + WhatsApp/phone) → "Para quem é" → "Como funciona" (numbered: avaliação com ecografia → plano → reabilitação no ginásio → critérios de regresso) → what is measured / equipment → space (ginásio 100 m², banho de gelo, sauna) → team (Mário, Angela) → duration/price if publishable → FAQ → closing booking block with WhatsApp + phone + map. Every reviewed premium page has some version of "how it works" as numbered steps; that is the section most often present across the sample (Six, Complete, Pivot, Melbourne CBD, HSU, Prehab, DDC).
- The sample suggests premium pages are narrower and deeper (Complete Physio: one test; Pivot/Melbourne CBD: one condition) while generic pages (Central Health, Fisio And Therapies, CUF) are wide lists with little process content.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve Pure Sports Medicine, Up and Running, Lifecare, Sports Focus Physio, Clínicas Espregueira (Clínica do Dragão) or Spanish premium clinics (e.g. CEMTRO, ReSport, Logik) service pages within the tool budget; search engines returned mostly directories (WhatClinic, Zaask, Doctoralia, TopDoctors).
- No boutique Portuguese (Lisbon/Porto/Algarve) sports-physio service page with a strong structure was found; CUF is a hospital-group template.

## 2. How are conditions presented (body area, injury type, goal)? Body-area selectors / human figures?

### Takeaway
None of the reviewed leading pages used an interactive human-body figure on the service page. Conditions are surfaced through (a) text cards by body area or situation, (b) stage/goal cards (rehab → readaptation → performance → return to sport), (c) a dedicated flagship condition page (ACL is the archetype), and (d) a short quiz ("Treatment Finder") instead of a body map.

### Cited Findings
- Central Health structures the service by goal/stage (7 cards: Injury Diagnosis → Injury Treatment → Injury Rehabilitation → Return to Sport → Performance Enhancement → Injury Prevention → Pain Management); body parts sit in the site navigation ("By Body Part" menu) and a separate "Self Help Tool", not on the page. — [Central Health](https://www.central-health.com/services/sports-physiotherapy)
- Six Physio uses a site-wide "Treatment Finder" quiz (header link + pop-up) rather than a body map; no body figure on the page. — [Six Physio](https://sixphysio.com/services/performance-physio)
- DDC Clinic's "O que está te incomodando?" uses 8 plain text cards mixing body area and situation: "Dor nas costas", "Joelho ou quadril", "Ombro ou punho", "Mandíbula (ATM)", "Dor de cabeça", "Lesão no esporte – Voltar a treinar", "Cirurgia – Pré e pós-operatório", "Mobilidade 60+"; plus a "Quiz de 1 minuto" (5 questions, explicitly framed as orientation that the assessment confirms). No body figure. — [DDC Clinic](https://ddcfisioterapia.com.br)
- Fisio And Therapies splits sports physio by goal (Rehabilitación de lesiones / Readaptación deportiva / Optimización del rendimiento / Valoración deportiva) and links condition articles (codo, rodilla, gemelo, ligamento cruzado anterior); its "Tratamientos" menu lists "Ecografía Muscular" as its own page. — [Fisio And Therapies](https://fisioandtherapies.com/fisioterapia-deportiva-alcala-de-henares/)
- HSU organises by stage of the injury journey: Prehabilitation, Post-Injury Rehabilitation, Sport-Specific Rehabilitation, Return-to-Sport Testing; no specific conditions listed. — [HSU Clinics](https://clinics.hsu.ac.uk/sports-rehabilitation-and-performance/)
- Complete Physio frames by population rather than condition: athletes post-surgery, people with multiple/recurrent injuries, recurring niggles. — [Complete Physio](https://complete-physio.co.uk/services/high-performance-testing-service)
- Condition-led flagship pages: Melbourne CBD has a full ACL page led by a PhD clinician; Pivot builds location-specific ACL pages (Ringwood, Bundoora). — [Melbourne CBD Physio](https://melbournecbdphysio.com.au/acl-physiotherapy/); [Pivot blog linking to location pages](https://pivotsportsperformance.com.au/post/acl-rehabilitation-knee-physio-strength-conditioning)
- CUF lists "Áreas de atuação" as a flat bullet list (Lesões Desportivas, Reintegração Desportiva, Intervenção Eco-guiada…). — [CUF](https://www.cuf.pt/hospitais-e-clinicas/hospital-cuf-porto/centros/unidade-de-medicina-desportiva-e-performance)

### Inferences
- If Fisio4you keeps a body map (the repo has components/BodyMap.tsx), it would be unusual relative to this sample; the more common premium pattern is text-first cards (body area + plain-language symptom line, like DDC's "Joelho ou quadril – Dor, lesão, desgaste") that each deep-link to a condition section/page. A body figure can still work as a secondary navigator, but the sample gives no evidence that leading clinics rely on it.
- Mixing body area with goals ("Voltar a treinar", "Pré e pós-operatório") reads closer to how patients self-identify than anatomical lists.
- An ACL / knee flagship section (or page) is the most copied pattern among sports clinics in this sample.

### Gaps
- No usability data found comparing body-map selectors vs. text cards for clinic sites. Sample of ~10 pages is small; body maps may exist on sites not reviewed.

## 3. How do they show the rehab journey and return-to-play testing/criteria?

### Takeaway
The best pages show a numbered, criteria-based (not time-based) pathway, name the actual tests (strength/dynamometry, force-plate jumps, hop tests, agility, psychological readiness questionnaires), and say what the patient receives at the end (a report shared with surgeon/coach). Timelines are given as ranges in FAQs, not promises.

### Cited Findings
- Six Physio RTS testing combines strength tests, hop and landing tasks, cognitively demanding tasks under fatigue, and psychological readiness. — [Six Physio](https://sixphysio.com/services/performance-physio)
- Complete Physio: "What to Expect in a Session" = History taking → Testing Session → Analysis & Feedback → Tailored Treatment Plan; tests named: Peak Force, Single-Leg Maximal Strength, Isometric Mid-Thigh Pull, Drop Jump, Countermovement Jump, Squat Jump; VALD force plates + fixed dynamometry; 90-minute session; references cited on-page. — [Complete Physio](https://complete-physio.co.uk/services/high-performance-testing-service)
- Pivot "How It Works" 8 steps: UNDERSTANDING YOU → CLINICAL ASSESSMENT → STRENGTH & POWER ASSESSMENT → JUMP & PLYOMETRIC ASSESSMENT → CAPACITY TESTING → PSYCHOLOGICAL READINESS → RETURN TO SPORT REPORT → YOUR FIRST GAME; offers a downloadable "Sample Return To Sport Report". — [Pivot Sports Performance](https://pivotsportsperformance.com.au/acl-rehabilitation-ringwood)
- Melbourne CBD: 5 criteria-based phases (Acute; Strengthening on Keiser/Kineo; Neuromuscular; Sport-specific; Return to sport) with RTS testing = limb symmetry index on Kineo, VALD hop testing, functional movement screening; FAQ states return to pivoting sport after reconstruction usually takes at least 9–12 months. — [Melbourne CBD Physio](https://melbournecbdphysio.com.au/acl-physiotherapy/)
- Melbourne CBD case-study article: ACL rehab "anywhere between 9-20 months", "criteria-driven"; recommends 8–12 weeks pre-surgery rehab; return-to-running screen = symptoms, lower-limb strength, questionnaires for psychological readiness, single-limb hop performance; uses Alter-G treadmill and VALD Nordbord; patient story with named athlete. (Article dated Feb 2021.) — [Melbourne CBD Physio blog](https://melbournecbdphysio.com.au/acl-rehab-journey-when-can-i-return-to-sport/)
- Prehab PT: subjective exam by a Doctor of Physical Therapy, then objective strength testing, force plate testing, 2D motion analysis, hop testing, agility testing; results explained test-by-test and shared with patient, surgeon, guardian and/or coach via a personalised patient profile. — [Prehab PT](https://clinic.theprehabguys.com/return-to-sport)
- HSU: RTS testing "against recognised benchmarks" using VALD ForceDecks and dynamometry (strength, power, limb asymmetries, balance, movement control). — [HSU Clinics](https://clinics.hsu.ac.uk/sports-rehabilitation-and-performance/)
- Sports & Exercise Physio (Brisbane) shows ACL phases with week ranges: Protect & Prepare (0–2 wks) → Strength & Mobility (2–8) → Neuromuscular & Balance (8–16) → Sport-specific (months 4–6) → Return to Sport (months 6–12). (Blog, Aug 2025.) — [Sports & Exercise Physio](https://sportsandexercise.physio/acl-injury-recovery-prevention-guide-to-a-safe-return/)
- DDC: generic 5-step "Da avaliação à alta" (Avaliação, Plano, Tratamento, Acompanhamento, Alta) — note the live page repeats the same placeholder description under steps 2–4, an example of how a weak process block undermines trust. — [DDC Clinic](https://ddcfisioterapia.com.br)
- Pivot frames integration as the differentiator: physio + strength & conditioning + change-of-direction + sprint mechanics "under one roof", with objective testing to guide RTS decisions. — [Pivot blog](https://pivotsportsperformance.com.au/post/acl-rehabilitation-knee-physio-strength-conditioning)

### Inferences
- For Fisio4you the natural pathway is: 1) Avaliação (história + exame + ecografia) → 2) Plano com objetivos → 3) Reabilitação no ginásio de 100 m² → 4) Testes/critérios de regresso → 5) Regresso ao treino/competição, with recovery (gelo/sauna) as a supporting element rather than a step. Only list tests/equipment the clinic actually performs (consistent with the "só factos confirmados" copy rule).
- "Criteria, not time" + a range-based FAQ answer ("quanto tempo demora?") is the honest pattern across the best examples.
- A sample report (PDF/image) is a strong, cheap trust device if Fisio4you produces any written discharge/RTS summary.

### Gaps
- Orlando Health's RTS testing page returned HTTP 429, so its criteria (e.g. LSI thresholds) could not be verified: https://www.orlandohealth.com/services/jewett-orthopedic-institute/centers-and-programs/rehabilitation-and-sports-medicine-center/return-to-sport-testing
- None of the fetched pages published numeric pass thresholds (e.g. LSI ≥90%) on the service page itself.

Video can be offered on click (consistent with the existing "vídeos só ao clicar" decision) since no leading page relied on autoplay video.
- Ice bath + sauna did not appear on any reviewed service page; it is a potential differentiator, but should be presented as recovery support, not as treatment claims.

### Gaps

- Design galleries (Awwwards, Siteinspire, Lapa Ninja, One Page Love) were not reviewed within the tool budget.

## 5. Where and how are CTAs placed (book, call, WhatsApp), and how many?

### Takeaway
Typical premium pages repeat one primary booking CTA 3–8 times at predictable points: sticky header, hero, after the "what to expect"/process block, on pricing cards, and in a closing block; phone is the standard secondary CTA; WhatsApp appears as a floating button (UK/Spain) or as the primary per-location channel with a prefilled message (Brazil). High-value services can be "enquire only" without hurting the page.

### Cited Findings
- Six Physio: "Book online" ~4× (header, hero, booking card, closing) + "Book now" 2× (booking section, RTS testing) + phone, email, floating WhatsApp button and footer WhatsApp icon. — [Six Physio](https://sixphysio.com/services/performance-physio)
- Complete Physio: "Book online" ~8× and phone ~6× (header, mobile bar, hero, booking section, card, footer); yet the testing service itself "cannot be booked online" and directs users to email. — [Complete Physio](https://complete-physio.co.uk/services/high-performance-testing-service)
- HSU: "Book" in header, "Book Now" under hero and after "Why Choose", "Book Online"/"Book an Assessment" in footer. — [HSU Clinics](https://clinics.hsu.ac.uk/sports-rehabilitation-and-performance/)
- Melbourne CBD: "book now" in nav + "Book Now" after intro and after the rehab-program section + "Contact us…" after FAQ. — [Melbourne CBD Physio](https://melbournecbdphysio.com.au/acl-physiotherapy/)
- Pivot: "Book Now" header, "Get Started" hero, "Book Appointment" on both pricing cards. — [Pivot Sports Performance](https://pivotsportsperformance.com.au/acl-rehabilitation-ringwood)
- Prehab PT: only 2 "Book Now" (nav + hero) + footer "Schedule". — [Prehab PT](https://clinic.theprehabguys.com/return-to-sport)
- Fisio And Therapies: "PEDIR CITA" in hero and two booking blocks; floating WhatsApp widget "Abrir chat"; phone in footer. — [Fisio And Therapies](https://fisioandtherapies.com/fisioterapia-deportiva-alcala-de-henares/)
- DDC Clinic (WhatsApp-first): "Agendar consulta"/"Agendar avaliação" open a unit selector that routes to WhatsApp; each unit card has "WhatsApp" + "Rota" buttons; WhatsApp links carry prefilled messages identifying the source (e.g. "…vim através do site da DDC Clinic - Unidade São Caetano e gostaria de ser atendido."); FAQ intro says "Chame a unidade mais perto no WhatsApp". — [DDC Clinic](https://ddcfisioterapia.com.br)
- Central Health: "Book Appointment" leads to a clinics page, plus enquiry form; no WhatsApp. — [Central Health](https://www.central-health.com/services/sports-physiotherapy)
- CUF: per-doctor "Marcação online" + "Marcar" per consultation type. — [CUF](https://www.cuf.pt/hospitais-e-clinicas/hospital-cuf-porto/centros/unidade-de-medicina-desportiva-e-performance)

### Inferences
- For a WhatsApp + phone only clinic: a paired CTA ("Marcar consulta" → WhatsApp with prefilled PT text naming the service, e.g. "Olá, vim do site e gostaria de marcar uma avaliação de fisioterapia desportiva", plus "Ligar") placed in header, hero, after "Como funciona", and in a closing block, i.e. ~4 placements, matches the sample's norm. A per-service prefilled message (DDC pattern) also tells the clinic which page converted.
- Complete Physio shows that a premium, non-instant booking path (email/enquiry) is acceptable when the page explains why; Fisio4you can frame WhatsApp as "resposta pessoal da equipa".

### Gaps
- No credible published conversion data (agency studies with numbers) on WhatsApp vs. online booking or CTA count for physio sites was found in this pass.

## 6. Concrete example pages (with what each does well)

Best models for Fisio4you: Pivot (process + pricing + proof on one flagship condition page), Complete Physio (naming tests/equipment with references), Melbourne CBD (authority-led, criteria-based ACL page), Six Physio (clean order, FAQ, floating WhatsApp), DDC (WhatsApp-first booking UX in Portuguese).
